00 / DECIDERS

Who was evaluated.

Every decider picks book, pace, sound, and visuals from RISE’s menus, validated by RISE’s production rules. Fixed labels carry no rank.

Reading the run file…

01 / AGREEMENT

Agreement with author expectations.

Measures how often admitted plans fall within author-written expectations (for example, 100 or 150 words per minute for a slow, restful request). Expectations met counts agreement across admitted plans; Opposites told apart counts distinct plans across paired opposite prompts. Author expectations are not objective truth, and prompts were tuned on Jev.

Reading the run file…

02 / CALIBRATION

Stated probability vs. observed agreement.

Reliability plots compare stated option probability (horizontal) against observed expectation agreement (vertical). Points on the dotted diagonal match; points below indicate overconfidence. Larger dots hold more choices; hollow dots indicate fewer than 15 samples.

03 / CASE BY CASE

Run 1 decisions per prompt.

Each cell shows the admitted plan: pace in words per minute, sound, visual mode and style, and book. Select any cell to replay that exact plan in RISE.

Reading the run file…

04 / ANSWERS, COST, TIME

Admission rates, latency, and billed cost.

Tracks admission counts, rejection reasons, response latency, and billed provider cost across all prompts and controls.

Reading the run file…

05 / METHOD

One frozen capture, one deterministic report.

The harness runs manually from RISE under scripts/arena/ using operator keys and spend caps (never in CI). Every answer passes RISE’s production checks, captures freeze into hash-named files, and CI verifies regenerated reports and replay files against the committed hashes below.
