Technical report · GC–01

GrokCell Execution: reliable execution for AI agents

Defines a six-operation execution contract (read, decide, call, check, admit, yield) enforcing permissions, budgets, validation, and crash recovery after model selection.

Status
  • Implemented single-machine Python/SQLite prototype with two offline fixtures.
  • Tested 87 passing regression tests and process-kill replay experiments (fresh replay, death during callback, death around admission).
  • Not yet live provider calls, verifier isolation, or task-quality and cost benchmarks.
Establishes
Crash-safe replay across process exit on two offline fixtures without resending interrupted steps under the same ID.
Does not establish
Live-provider quality, adversarial isolation (the verifier runs as trusted code), remote exactly-once semantics, power-loss durability, or live latency and cost.
Date
26 September 2026 · revision 1

Research note · SB–01

Sybershoke: shock tests for multi-agent systems

Deterministic fault-injection harness using seeded fault plans, history-only invariant checks, and failure shrinking. Revision 2 tests the live RISE Worker source against 39 captured production Jev answers.

Status
  • Measured Week 0 simulator: 43 tests, 4/4 seeded bugs caught, 0 failures over 12,000 runs (2,000 seeds per campaign).
  • Tested 89 Jev/Kev model tests following an 11-finding adversarial red-team review.
  • Measured RISE Worker source on 39 captured production Jev answers: 10 pre-registered predictions (1 refuted); caught a 300 wpm sleep-override bug and missing provider fallback with replayable seeds.
  • Not yet live traffic tests, Kev captures, or publication of the Week 0 repo.
Establishes
Reproducible detection of seeded bugs and two replayable defects in RISE Worker source (override fix merged in SyberLabs/RISE#306).
Does not establish
Production failure rates (model counts depend on assumed parameters, so only caught vs. missed holds), Kev behavior, or live production behavior (in-memory stand-ins and virtual time). Sound-loudness ranks reflect adapter heuristics.
Date
29 September 2026 · revision 2

Working paper · internal draft

DTBR-MC: the marker is not the binding constraint

Falsification-oriented Monte Carlo audit of deep-time hazard warnings. Shows physical access and consequence act as hard gates while symbolic markers only modulate behavior; disproves the additive capacity-threshold hypothesis algebraically.

Status
  • Implemented seeded vectorized simulator, H1/sensitivity/H3 experiments, and initial output bundle.
  • Tested deterministic regression and falsifiability suite in tests/.
  • Not yet external review and primary-source citation audit.
Establishes
Which modeling assumptions are load-bearing once warning design is written as explicit equations, with every output epistemically labeled.
Does not establish
Distant-future human behavior, operational nuclear-waste policy, or predictive forecasts. Audits model consistency only.
Date
26 September 2026

Empirical artifact

Grokking Scaling Theory

Evaluates modular-arithmetic grokking scaling laws via model-fit competition across 21 scaling points and 63 bit-deterministic runs (5 architectures).

Status
  • Measured 21 scaling points (19 measured) and 63 bit-deterministic Phase 2 runs across 5 architectures (18/18 seed reproducibility verified).
  • Implemented fit competition, corrected scaling law, RG-inspired flow models, and sheaf and logical-cell instruments.
  • Not yet additional measured runs, full tests, and manuscript polish.
Establishes
Sheaf gluing beats variance baselines by 10 to 25% lead time, no logical cells assemble, width dominates quantization pressure over depth, and pooled fits point to regime dependence over universality.
Does not establish
A complete theory of grokking or architecture-independent universal scaling laws.

Interim findings

Vital Language: coherence, agency and literary vitality

Tests whether chaotic logit modulation improves prose vitality on Qwen2.5-0.5B and 1.5B. Hypothesis inverted: token-level chaos does not beat plain sampling on vitality proxies, while a persistent-speaker prompt scaffold eliminated degenerate outputs.

Status
  • Measured CPU epsilon sweeps (6 prompts × 3 seeds): white noise pushes self-perplexity from 7.8 to 176 while matched chaos stays near 10; the agency scaffold yielded 0/18 degenerate passages vs. 3/18 for plain sampling.
  • Implemented generation harness with chaotic, matched-noise, and white-noise controls, MFDFA, coherence metrics, and blind rating scaffold.
  • Not yet independent human raters.
Establishes
Rater-independent coherence and degeneracy counts, plus sensitivity of MFDFA on published literature (shuffle surrogates collapse Joyce’s width while barely shifting Austen’s).
Does not establish
Felt-vitality claims: human-judgment data is n = 1 from the assistant model itself, leaving all vitality claims as unverified hypotheses.
Date
Interim · published 26 September 2026
Source
