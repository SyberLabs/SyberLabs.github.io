Privacy

What this site does with your data.

syberlabs.io is a static website. Visitors get no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. The one exception is a staff sign-in at /admin/ for people SyberLabs adds.

Hosting
The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. As with any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver pages and protect the service.
Fonts
Pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google’s servers.
Video
The RISE demo page embeds a video from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play on that video. The site’s own demo video is served from syberlabs.io.
Email
Contact links open your email app. SyberLabs receives only what you choose to send and uses it to reply to you.
Staff sign-in
People SyberLabs adds can sign in with GitHub, and can add Google. We receive the account’s numeric id and username from GitHub and keep them, with the roles we grant and sign-in times, in a Cloudflare database. For Google, we receive the account’s id and email address. Sessions and admin actions are recorded with a shortened IP address and the browser name. We never keep Google or GitHub access tokens. Starting a sign-in sets a 10-minute cookie on syberlabs.io; signing in sets a session cookie that expires after 13 hours, and the session itself ends after 12. Nothing is set for visitors who don’t start a sign-in, nothing is recorded about people who are refused, and nothing reaches rise., omni. or sketch.syberlabs.io. Records are kept for about 400 days. Staff accounts are turned off, not deleted, when access ends; ask us and we will remove your details. Processors: GitHub, Google, Cloudflare.
Other sites
Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, TypeSafe AI and other sites follow those sites’ own policies. The RISE reading app has its own RISE privacy policy.
