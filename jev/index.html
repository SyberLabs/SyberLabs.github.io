LIVE READING CHOICE

One request.
Many ways to read.

Describe the reading you want on the RISE home screen. Jev chooses a released text and passage, then sets the Chamber. RISE now offers 23 browser-synthesized sound choices: gentle piano and jazz, six more keyboard styles from lullaby to ragtime, six cinematic themes, six mood beds, rain, and two ambient pieces. RISE validates every choice before playback.

Postgres stores the sound choices and Redis caches them briefly. Jev sees a compact shortlist matched to the request, so the larger catalog does not require a larger model menu. Seven text faces and five sizes broaden the visual treatment. Repeated open-ended requests rotate reading and presentation hints. The separate Scriptorium composition route still needs an independently verified authoring outcome.
