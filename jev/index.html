AI decisions with clear limits.

Structured AI answers. Rules in code. The reader stays in control.

See how it works

An independent SyberLabs integration of Jev, a decision API from TypeSafe AI, in the RISE reading app. SyberLabs has no partnership with TypeSafe AI.

How Jev answers

Every AI decision has a fixed format.

Jev answers three kinds of questions: choose one option, estimate the probability of a yes-or-no condition, or rate on a scale. Select a type to see an example. The application’s own code still decides what is allowed.

In the live app

One request. Many ways to read.

On the RISE home screen, readers describe the reading they want. Jev chooses a released text and passage, then configures the reader. RISE offers 23 sound options synthesized in the browser: gentle piano and jazz, six other keyboard styles from lullaby to ragtime, six cinematic themes, six mood beds, rain, and two ambient pieces. RISE validates every choice before playback.

Sound options are stored in PostgreSQL and briefly cached in Redis. Jev receives a short list matched to each request, so a growing catalog does not enlarge the set of options it must choose from. Seven typefaces and five text sizes add visual range, and repeated open-ended requests rotate reading and presentation suggestions. A separate AI composition feature, the Scriptorium, has not yet been independently verified.
