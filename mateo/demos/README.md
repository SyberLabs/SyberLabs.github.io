# Demonstration media

- `gyre-motion-*.mp4` and their posters are cinematic adaptations of the mathematical rendering in [Mateo's Gyre](https://sykosyber.github.io/engines/works/gyre.html). They use its Lorenz equations and RK4 integration with a periodic camera. They are artwork, not recordings of the product interface. Each clip is 16 seconds, contains no audio and is under 0.75 MB.
- `gyre-orbit.mp4` records an actual browser interaction with Gyre, including a new starting pattern.
- `rise-playback.mp4` records the RISE reading product from the 2026-10-05 build at `SyberLabs/RISE@998d725`. It demonstrates the product, rather than proving a particular later change.

The GitHits evidence in `../proof/` uses controlled test data. It does not establish customer acceptance, adoption or savings.

## Gyre to RISE Sophia

`gyre-sophia-*.mp4` uses full-canvas Gyre motion and the actual RISE Sophia shell geometry. The rendering keeps the room ground transparent so Gyre remains visible behind the shell. These are cinematic adaptations of the public visual code, not a live application or model request.

Earth imagery: NASA/GSFC, [NASA catalog source](https://images.nasa.gov/details/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e002131). The visible website credit links to the same source. This is factual imagery attribution, not a NASA endorsement.

RISE source license and notices are preserved in `RISE-LICENSE.txt` and `RISE-NOTICE.txt`. The background files contain no audio; the approved narrated pitch is separate.

## Homepage reveal

The homepage hero uses `gyre-sophia-v1.mp4` and its portrait variant alone. Its
first-visit reveal is timed to the clip in `src/hero-media.js`; no other intro
media is used.

## Sophia frame pictures and lab artwork

`../frame/` holds the pictures shown in the hero's Sophia frame in place of
Earth: *The Pink Cloud*, Henri-Edmond Cross, c. 1896, open access (CC0) at the
Cleveland Museum of Art (2020.106), and two pieces by Mateo Robles made with
Midjourney, *Dune Run* and *Chromatic Burst*. `../lab/` holds his agent network
and chrome emblem, also made with Midjourney (the network's baked-in caption is
cropped off). Every piece is credited on the homepage.
