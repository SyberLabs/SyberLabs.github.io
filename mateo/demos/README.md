# Demonstration media

- `gyre-motion-*.mp4` and their posters are cinematic adaptations of the mathematical rendering in [Mateo's Gyre](https://sykosyber.github.io/engines/works/gyre.html). They use its Lorenz equations and RK4 integration with a periodic camera. They are artwork, not recordings of the product interface. Each clip is 16 seconds, contains no audio and is under 0.75 MB.
- `gyre-orbit.mp4` records an actual browser interaction with Gyre, including a new starting pattern.
- `rise-playback.mp4` records the RISE reading product from the 2026-10-05 build at `SyberLabs/RISE@998d725`. It demonstrates the product, rather than proving a particular later change.

The GitHits evidence in `../proof/` uses controlled test data. It does not establish customer acceptance, adoption or savings.
