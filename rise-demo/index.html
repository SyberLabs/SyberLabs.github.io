01 / RISE × Jev: request to playbackLive product capture · 87 seconds

A generated presenter narrates a real capture of the public RISE flow. The demo asks for Meditations with psychedelic, fast Tokyo Drift energy; RISE treats that phrase as a style cue, then Jev shows the interpretation before playback. The reader adjusts energy, speed, color, sound, and text locally before pressing play.

No film footage, cars, or copyrighted soundtrack are used. The presenter and voice are AI-generated, the music bed is original, and the product interaction shown on screen is the live RISE experience.
