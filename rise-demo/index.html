RISE

A reading app that moves text
through time and across space.

Interactive sample / Middlemarch

Change the scene while you read.

Read a two-scene sample in RISE. During playback, press Shift scene to bring the next visual forward. The words and your reading place stay put.

Open interactive sample Source: Middlemarch by George Eliot, Standard Ebooks. This preset does not call the Jev API.
01 / Product demo: Stream, Page, and visual sequenceScreen recording and concept sequence · 83 seconds

A screen recording of RISE’s Stream and Page modes, followed by a concept sequence with simulated Jev routing: a kaleidoscopic attractor, a fractal flame, a tiger print by Ohara Koson, and astronomy imagery. Tiger image: Rijksmuseum, RP-P-1999-385 (public domain).

New in the live app: ask for a reading that feels sad, angry, happy, excited, thrilling, or scary, and Jev chooses an existing passage and a matching sound. The recorded Stream and Page segment predates this feature, and the visual-routing sequence is simulated.

Visual sources: John James Audubon, Wild Turkey (public domain); Jupiter and Europa © NASA, ESA, A. Simon, M. H. Wong and the OPAL team, CC BY 4.0.
