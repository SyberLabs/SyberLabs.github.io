SUBSCRIPTION

Managing Plus

Availability, checkout, and billing run through the RISE Plus page inside the app. Once sales open, checkout returns you directly to RISE.

Keep your checkout email address for account recovery, and review final pricing, renewal, and cancellation terms before paying.

DATA HANDLING

Where your text goes

Selected text routes through SyberLabs to ElevenLabs for audio synthesis. Generated audio and playback timings store privately for up to 30 days for cross-device replay. Stripe processes payments; usage is metered.

See the RISE privacy notice and terms for full service details.
