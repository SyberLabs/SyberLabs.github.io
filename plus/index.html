THE EXPERIENCE

Read along. Or just listen.

Plus is for text you bring or compose in RISE. The voice workspace takes up to 5,000 characters at a time, with up to 25,000 characters of new narration per day. Choose among five voices, then replay the audio saved in that browser.

The allowance counts characters sent for new narration, including spaces. That is roughly 10,000 words, depending on the text. Replaying saved audio in the same browser does not create a new narration. Generating it again, including on another device or after clearing browser data, uses the allowance again.

When your allowance is used up, new Plus narration pauses until the next billing period. Free reading continues. Service-wide capacity limits can also temporarily pause new narration.

YOUR SUBSCRIPTION

One place for Plus.

The RISE Plus page in the app is the starting point for availability, subscription access and billing. Once sales open, it will take you through checkout and back to RISE.

Keep the email address you use at checkout: subscription recovery uses that address.

Review the final price, renewal and cancellation terms at checkout before paying.

YOUR TEXT

You choose what gets a voice.

When you request Plus narration, the selected text passes through SyberLabs to ElevenLabs to generate audio. The audio is saved in your browser; the RISE service does not store a copy of the text or audio. Stripe handles payment details. Subscription usage is metered.

See the RISE privacy notice and terms for the service’s full details.
