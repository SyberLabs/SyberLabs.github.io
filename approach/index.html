OUR APPROACH

Make the work observable.

We build software that lets people steer what AI does and check the result.

See the projects

HOW WE BUILD

Start with what is true.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Establish what the system actually knows and can do right now.

  2. 02

    Design

    Decide what a person should be able to see, choose, or approve.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Test that people can steer the experience and check the result.
