OUR APPROACH

Our approach: observe, design, verify.

We build software that lets people steer what AI does and check the result.

See the products

HOW WE BUILD

The three steps

  1. 01

    Observe

    Establish what the system knows and can do right now.

  2. 02

    Design

    Decide what a person should be able to see, choose, or approve.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Test that a person can direct the system and check its result.

We apply the same steps to our tools: our coding agents read dependencies at the exact version a project uses. How we build covers that, and what it sends off our machines.
