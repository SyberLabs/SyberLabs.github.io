# Demonstration media

- `gyre-full-v1.mp4` and `gyre-full-portrait-v1.mp4`, with their posters, are the homepage's muted background
  clips: cinematic renderings of the Lorenz equations (RK4 integration, periodic camera). They are artwork, not
  recordings of a product interface. They contain no audio.
- `rise-playback.mp4` records the RISE reading product from the 2026-10-05 build at `SyberLabs/RISE@998d725`. It
  demonstrates the product rather than proving a particular later change.

The GitHits evidence in `../proof/` uses controlled test data. It does not establish customer acceptance, adoption
or savings.

RISE source license and notices are preserved in `RISE-LICENSE.txt` and `RISE-NOTICE.txt`.
