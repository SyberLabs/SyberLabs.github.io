# Demonstration media

- `gyre-full-v1.mp4` and `gyre-full-portrait-v1.mp4`, with their posters, were the homepage's muted background
  clips until November 18, 2026: cinematic renderings of the Lorenz equations (RK4 integration, periodic camera).
  No page plays them now. They are kept so that first screen can be restored (CLAUDE.md, "Homepage"), and the
  build still checks that the first one is published. They are artwork, not recordings of a product interface.
  They contain no audio.
- `rise-playback.mp4` records the RISE reading product from the 2026-10-05 build at `SyberLabs/RISE@998d725`. It
  demonstrates the product rather than proving a particular later change.

The GitHits evidence in `../proof/` uses controlled test data. It does not establish customer acceptance, adoption
or savings.

RISE source license and notices are preserved in `RISE-LICENSE.txt` and `RISE-NOTICE.txt`.
