RISE / AI integration / September 2026

Moving to Kev.

RISE is migrating its bounded reading decisions from Jev to Kev. The migration is in progress; we have not confirmed a live Kev reading or measured Kev on RISE requests.

Explore Kev upstream ↗

CURRENT STATUS

What the evidence says.

  1. 01 / ESTABLISHED

    Bounded decisions in RISE

    RISE offers a defined menu of reading choices, validates the answer in application code, and lets the reader override the result. The recorded integration and preference evaluation used Jev.

  2. 02 / IN PROGRESS

    Kev integration

    The RISE migration is adding a server-side, pinned Kev provider configuration for that decision path. Kev is Jared Palmer’s open project for small decision models with a compatible typed API.

  3. 03 / UNVERIFIED

    Live behavior

    We have not confirmed a production Kev deployment, a successful live Kev reading, or an independent RISE-specific Kev evaluation. Jev’s earlier 48 of 49 preference result is not a Kev result.
