Process

Four steps from intro to handover.

  1. Intro call. Align on the problem, timeline, and acceptance criteria.
  2. Written scope. Fixed scope, schedule, and quote in writing before work begins.
  3. Verified build. Iterative delivery via reviewed changes backed by passing checks and execution records.
  4. Handover. Full code, documentation, and audit records under the IP and licensing terms set in scope.
