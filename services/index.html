SYBERLABS / READING PILOTS FOR PUBLISHERS

An interactive way to read your text.

Give readers a new way into your next publication. For independent presses and literary magazines, we offer paid pilots that turn one rights-cleared poem or excerpt into an interactive browser reading, built on RISE.

Experience a sample Discuss an excerpt pilot

One text. One reader journey.

FIRST / TRY THE READING

Words become an experience.

Open a passage from George Eliot’s Middlemarch in RISE. Press Start sample reading and see the words unfold through a visual scene. Pause whenever you like.

Open the interactive sample

Free. No account or AI connection needed. This is a fixed product demonstration, not a customer commission.

Text source: Standard Ebooks’ edition of Middlemarch. Prefer to watch? See the 62-second RISE demo.

01 / YOUR TEXT

Start with one excerpt.

A poem, a passage from an upcoming book, or a selection from an issue. Tell us who it is for and what you want readers to experience. You must control the rights needed to share and use the text.

02 / THE PILOT

Shape one reading experience.

A pilot can use RISE’s Page (spatial layout) and Stream (paced) reading modes, with presentation choices suited to the text. We define the deliverable together after reviewing your material.

03 / THE BOUNDARY

Keep the first build small.

One text and one reader journey. Rights clearance, manuscript editing, accounts, payments and custom AI work are outside this pilot. Bring a reading goal; we will discuss whether RISE fits it.

WHAT A PILOT PRODUCES

One reading of your text, ready to share.

For a publisher, magazine or creator: one excerpt, made into a RISE reading and handed back as a link your readers can open. The Middlemarch sample above shows what a reader gets.

01 / DELIVERABLES

What you get.

  • One excerpt of your text as a RISE reading: paced for the text, with visuals and sound chosen and styled for your publication, in Stream (paced) and Page (spatial) views.
  • A shareable browser link. Readers need no account and no install; it opens in any modern browser.
  • The same link ready for your site, newsletter or social posts. An embedded frame on your own page is scoped case by case.
  • A short note after launch on what readers did with it. RISE has no reader analytics, so the note draws on your own link and page counts and what readers tell you.
02 / TIMELINE

Four steps.

  1. We review your excerpt and goal, then send scope, dates and a fixed quote.
  2. You get a draft link to read and comment on.
  3. One round of revisions, then the final link.
  4. After readers have had it, the short note.

Dates are agreed in the quote, against your own launch if you have one.

03 / FROM YOU

What we need.

  • The excerpt as text, and confirmation that you hold the rights to share it this way.
  • Your publication’s look: a link to your site, a logo, colours or a page to match.
  • Who it is for and where you will share it.
  • One person to review the draft.

A spoken voice is optional. The RISE Plus premium voice (ElevenLabs) is built; paid launch pending verification.

NEXT / A SHORT CONVERSATION

Tell us about your publication.

A short, nonconfidential brief is enough to start.

Discuss an excerpt pilot

Or email syberlabs.software@gmail.com. You can select and copy the address.

Include in your first email

  1. Your publication’s name and public website.
  2. The readers you want to reach and your reading or launch goal.
  3. The kind and approximate length of text, plus the rights you hold or approval still needed.
  4. Your timing, if you have a date in mind.

The button opens a draft in your email app if configured. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. Please do not attach an unpublished manuscript or sensitive personal information in the first message. Your email provider handles delivery; SyberLabs uses your message to discuss the request.
