How an engagement works

What are you building?

  1. Intro call. A short call about the problem, the timing and what success looks like.
  2. Written scope. Scope, dates and a quote in writing before any work starts.
  3. Build with evidence. Work arrives as reviewed changes, each with the checks it passed and a record of how it was made.
  4. Handover. Code, documentation and records are delivered to you; ownership and licensing are set in the written scope.

RISE reading pilot

For a text-based experience, the RISE pilot covers one excerpt, Stream and Page views, a shareable link, one revision and a short post-launch note. Dates and a fixed quote follow review.

You provide the necessary text rights. Editing, rights clearance, accounts, payments and custom AI are outside that pilot. Embedding and optional spoken voice are scoped separately. RISE has no reader analytics; the note uses your own link and page counts and reader feedback.
