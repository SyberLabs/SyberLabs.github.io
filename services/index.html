SYBERLABS / SERVICES

An interactive way to read your text.

SyberLabs offers paid pilots that turn one rights-cleared excerpt of your text into a focused, interactive reading experience in the browser, built on RISE.

Ask about a pilot Experience RISE

We review the text and goal before agreeing on scope, schedule, and a fixed quote.

01 / INPUT

Start with one excerpt.

Tell us what you publish, who reads it, and what you want them to experience. You must control the rights needed to share and use the text.

02 / PILOT

Shape one reading experience.

A pilot can use RISE’s Page (spatial layout) and Stream (paced) reading modes, with presentation choices suited to the text. We define the deliverable together after reviewing your material.

03 / BOUNDARY

Keep the first build small.

The initial conversation is about one text and one reader journey. Rights clearance, manuscript editing, accounts, payments, and custom AI work are outside this pilot.

NEXT STEP

Tell us what readers should experience.

In your first email, include your organization, the kind and approximate length of text, the rights you hold, the intended readers, and your goal. A short, nonconfidential description is enough to start.
