Engagement

How an engagement works

  1. Intro call. A short call about the problem, the timing and how you will judge the result.
  2. Written scope. Scope, dates and a quote in writing before any work starts.
  3. Build. Work arrives as reviewed changes, each with the checks it passed and a record of how it was made.
  4. Handover. You receive the code, documentation and records. Ownership and licensing are set in the written scope.

What to put in the first email

A nonconfidential outline of the problem, scope and timing. The button opens a draft in your email app, and you choose whether to send it. Your email provider handles delivery. SyberLabs uses the message only to discuss your request. Privacy policy →

RISE reading pilot

For a text-based experience, the RISE pilot covers one excerpt, Stream and Page views, a shareable link, one revision and a short post-launch note. Dates and a fixed quote follow review.

You provide the necessary text rights. Editing, rights clearance, accounts, payments and custom AI are outside that pilot. Embedding and optional spoken voice are scoped separately. RISE has no reader analytics; the note uses your own link and page counts and reader feedback.

Attest documentation pilot
